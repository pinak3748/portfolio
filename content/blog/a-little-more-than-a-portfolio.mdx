I don't want this to be just a place where I put my work.

I want it to be a place where I share how I think, what I'm building, what I'm learning, and sometimes, what I'm getting wrong.

I'm curious about design. I like seeing how products are changing, playing with new ideas, and trying to understand why something works. Sometimes that means taking a design trend and trying it in a product I'm building. Sometimes it means making a small experiment just to see what happens.

Not everything will work. That's kind of the point.

I also like building things myself. Design, code, product I enjoy being somewhere in the middle of all three. Building something usually teaches me much more than just thinking about it.

So you'll probably find a mix of things here: work I've done, things I'm experimenting with, ideas I'm exploring, and things I'm learning along the way.

I don't really have a fixed destination for this.

I just want to keep building, keep trying new things, and keep getting a little better with every project.

That's what this place is for.
